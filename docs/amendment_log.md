### 23. 2026-09-10 (before any M2 fit) — Stage E infrastructure and evaluation

*written before the result*

**Change.** *Backend:* PyMC 6.3 models sampled with nutpie 0.16, 4 chains, 1,000 tuning and 1,000 draws, in a separate environment (`.venv-m2`). Every random effect is non-centred. *Prior predictive checks* (500 draws) run before a rung's first fit. A rung is fitted only if, for every ICB, the central 98% of its prior-predictive monthly counts lies within [observed minimum ÷ 10, observed maximum × 10]; otherwise priors are rescaled and the change recorded here. *Structure:* Type 1 attendances and other attendances (all types minus Type 1) are modelled as two negative-binomial series; all-types attendances are their sum, draw by draw. Admissions are negative-binomial with Type 1 attendances as the exposure: fitted on the observed series, forecast on the simulated one, so attendance uncertainty propagates. Each series is centred on the log of its ICB's mean over the 12 months before the origin, and time is measured in years from the origin. *Evaluation:* as-of, ICB level, the 35 even-month DEV origins (2018-04, 2018-06, …, 2023-12), horizons 1–6, all three targets. Comparators are ETS and the Stage D selection, run at ICB level with the same calibration method, pooled over ICBs. *Retention rule (work order):* a rung is kept only if it improves winter-h3 WIS (geometric mean over targets) by more than the Stage A minimum meaningful difference **and** reduces the worst assessable-cell |cov90 − 0.90|. ### 24. 2026-09-10 (before any M2 fit) — M2a, the registered specification at ICB level

*written before the result*

### 28. 2026-09-10 (before any M2d fit) — M2d, random-walk level

*written before the result*

**Change.** Built on M2a, the highest retained rung: shared Fourier seasonality, global dispersion per series, admissions with Type 1 attendances as exposure. The ICB intercept, linear slope and COVID flag are replaced by a random-walk level per ICB and series. For s ∈ {Type 1, other, admissions}: log μ = offset + ℓ_s,i,t + Fourier_s + d_s·booked-era (+ log Type 1 attendances for admissions). ℓ at the first month ~ N(0, 0.5); ℓ_t = ℓ_t−1 + η with η ~ Student-t(ν = 4, 0, σ_rw,s) and σ_rw,s ~ HalfNormal(0.03); centred (the levels are the sampled coordinates). For admissions, ℓ is the level of the log admission rate per Type 1 attendance, so M2e's remaining increment is the winter term on conversion. The booked-era flag stays, as a known definitional change. The COVID flag goes, and the walk absorbs COVID; no Type 3 → UTC flag exists in the pipeline. Forecasts carry the level forward by sampling future innovations, so uncertainty grows with horizon. *Comparison base:* M2a refitted with centred ICB effects (`m2a_c`, the same model as M2a with better-mixed chains) on the same 35 origins. Retention by the work order's rule against `m2a_c`; the non-centred M2a fits and the decisions already taken stay as reported. Prior-predictive gate as for M2a.

**Reason.** Student-t rather than Gaussian innovations: a Gaussian walk could absorb the April 2020 collapse only by inflating σ_rw, which would widen every later interval. Heavy tails let a few months move a long way. ν is fixed at 4, not estimated, to keep the posterior well identified.

### 29. 2026-09-10 (after diagnostic M2d fits at one origin, before any M2d backtest) — M2d drops the booked-era flag

*written before the result · Ellie's decision*

**Change.** (a deviation from the work order, which says to keep it). With a heavy-tailed random-walk level, the level after August 2020 is identified only as walk plus flag. ### 30. 2026-09-10 (same) — M2 data rule from M2d on (applied to `m2a_c` too, so the comparison differs only in the level model)

**Change.** An ICB-month of any series that is ≤ 0 or below 10% of that series' trailing 12-month median is treated as missing. Other attendances are all types minus Type 1 at ICB level, and all types sum only providers with complete returns. The result can be near zero for months at a time: ICB QVV reports exactly zero from June 2022 to March 2023, then about 3,800. A static trend absorbed such months as noise; a random walk follows them to e⁻¹¹. The 10% threshold leaves the COVID collapse (30–60% of trend) as data. A 50% rule would have flagged 142 ICB-months, 92 of them genuine COVID months.

**Reason.** Same diagnostic fits. The target definition for scoring is unchanged.

### 31. 2026-09-10 (after further diagnostic M2d fits, before any M2d backtest) — M2d: boundary-avoiding priors for the two noise scales

*written before the result*

**Change.** Dispersion κ_s ~ Gamma(4, 200) (mean 0.02, density zero at zero), replacing HalfNormal(0.1). Random-walk scale σ_rw,s ~ Gamma(4, 100) (mean 0.04), replacing HalfNormal(0.03). M2a and `m2a_c` keep their priors.

**Reason.** After the two changes above, a diagnostic fit at 2023-12 still failed (R-hat 1.78). Three chains put the other-attendance dispersion κ at about 0.017; one found a second mode at 0.002, where the walk tracks every monthly wiggle and observation noise vanishes. That is the signal-versus-noise trade-off of local-level models. HalfNormal priors put their highest density at zero, which favours that degenerate corner; priors that vanish at zero are the standard remedy.

### 32. 2026-09-10 (after further diagnostic M2d fits, before any M2d backtest) — From `m2a_c` and M2d on, all-types attendances are modelled directly

*written before the result*

**Change.** as their own negative-binomial series with their own level, instead of Type 1 + other. Coherence (all types ≥ Type 1) is no longer guaranteed by construction and is checked in the forecasts instead.

**Reason.** E0's decomposition derives other attendances as all types minus Type 1 at ICB level. That difference inherits both aggregates' reporting artefacts, and it does not match how all-types is scored (summed over providers with complete returns). With it, M2d's other-attendance dispersion stayed bimodal (R-hat 1.27–1.47 at two of three diagnostic origins) even with the priors above. Modelling all types directly gave R-hat 1.010, 1.033 and 1.084 at origins 2019-10, 2020-10 and 2023-12, with no divergences. This is the last change before the M2d backtest; the diagnostic fits behind the four M2d amendments are described in these rows and produced no scores.

### 33. 2026-09-10 (after the M2d backtest) — Retention rule overridden for M2d

### 40. 2026-09-10 (before it is run) — `m2d_corr`: M2d with correlated ICB innovations after fitting (Ellie's option 1, replacing M2f for the frozen M2)

### 82. 2026-09-10 (before any Stage D result) — Stage D design

*written before the result*

**Change.** *Base forecasts:* `m1_v3_raw` (v3 trained on every row, no in-model conformal step) and ETS (B1), as-of, provider level, origins 2017-07 to 2023-12; 2017-07 to 2018-03 are burn-in (calibration history only, never scored). `m1_v3` with its in-model pooled CQR is the status-quo reference. *Common rules:* calibration works on the log1p scale, per target, horizon and central interval (50/80/90/95%); the median is left alone; adjusted quantiles are sorted and floored at zero. A forecast from origin *t′* for month *P* enters calibration only at the first origin whose as-of data contain *P* (so at horizon *h* no earlier than *t′ + h*), using the first-published outturn; scoring stays against the latest revision. *Methods:* (1) **pooled split conformal**: CQR scores of the 12 most recent resolved forecast origins at that horizon, pooled across providers; (2) **per-series**: the provider's own scores over its 36 most recent resolved origins, used only when n ≥ ⌈1/a⌉ for an interval of miscoverage *a* (otherwise pooled), shrunk as e = (n·ê + κ·e_pool)/(n + κ) with κ = 12; (3) **DtACI** (Gibbs & Candès): per provider × horizon × interval, eight experts with γ ∈ {0.001, 0.002, …, 0.128}, deterministic weighted-average output, pinball-loss weights on β, σ = 1/(2I), the paper's adaptive η over the last I resolved steps, I = 60; it adapts the level at which the pooled score distribution is read; α ≤ 0 reads the largest pooled score; (4) **EnbPI** (Xu & Xie): 20 LightGBM median models per horizon (v3 features, tuned hyperparameters) on bootstraps of non-overlapping 12-month blocks of training months; out-of-bag residuals of the latest 12 target months, pooled across providers; quantiles = ensemble mean + empirical residual quantiles (LightGBM only). *Candidates (10):* `m1_v3`; `m1_v3_raw` × {raw, pooled, per-series, DtACI}; ETS × {raw, pooled, per-series, DtACI}; EnbPI. *Evaluation:* DEV, as-of, provider level; coverage by origin-year × horizon in the assessable region defined above; winter horizon-3 WIS per target; Pareto front on (geometric mean over targets of winter-h3 WIS relative to raw ETS, worst assessable-cell |cov90 − 0.90|). *Selection (STOP 4 rule):* among candidates meeting the acceptance criterion, the lowest downstream decision loss (defined in the Stage G amendment, computed after G's occupancy check); if none meets it, the smallest worst-cell deviation, with ties inside the Stage A minimum meaningful difference broken by WIS; if G's occupancy check fails its tolerance, selection falls back to WIS among criterion-meeting candidates. Any divergence between the decision-loss choice and the WIS choice is reported as a finding.

**Reason.** Written before any calibrated forecast exists, so that the method, windows, shrinkage constant and selection rule cannot be tuned to the DEV result. ETS is included because it is the operational candidate; adaptive methods need delayed feedback because an h-step interval is only checkable h months later.

### 83. 2026-09-10 (before any Stage G result) — Stage G design; §8's length-of-stay rule replaced

*written before the result*

**Change.** *Occupancy model:* for trust j and quarter q, log(O·D/A) = log c_j + ε, where A is emergency admissions via A&E in the quarter, D its days and O KH03 average daily occupied G&A beds, so c_j is effective occupied bed-days per A&E admission (it absorbs elective and GP-direct admissions, case mix, SDEC coding and discharge delay as a ratio).
