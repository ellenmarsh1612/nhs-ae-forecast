Nothing in the register blocks the tag. Three items were Ellie's to decide at STOP 9 (`docs/stage_h_stop9.md`), and she decided them on 2026-09-15 (§10 row "STOP 9 decisions"): D-1, the training-residual exception, confirmed and closed to a list; D-20, Künsch kept; D-21, 4 or more of the 19 counted units. ## Attention (8)

1. **D-1** (flag 1). This is the only place where the seal knowingly lets residuals on sealed periods be computed before the unseal. The P12 row asks Ellie to confirm it, and "fitting in general" should become a closed list.
2. **D-20.** The primary's verdict is read off this interval, and the 2025-07 unit gets about a third of an interior origin's weight. Either accept that knowingly or switch to circular blocks, which means a code change, updated tests and a new dry run before the tag.
3. **D-21.** This decides whether H2's M2 clause gets a verdict at all. The design's "4 of 19" and the plan's "more than 20% of origins" (5 of 21) disagree at exactly four failures, and the CONF fits are expected to be the worst sampled.
4. **D-22** (flag 3). The label "§9 fallback" claims a registered trigger that never fired, and the ICB fits themselves exceed §5's R-hat bar at late origins. Relabel at plan L199 and prereg L244.
5. **D-46** (flag 2). Once tagged, the plan's §3 would misstate the lost-originals periods and contradict the H4b row, and the split is empty on CONF19. One line to fix at plan L203.
6. **D-39** (flag 4). The P10 row's sentence does not match the code. At 2018-11 and 2021-10, H4's rows do not pair, so two of the five DEV seasons behind H4's range lose an origin without saying so.
7. **D-23, D-25, D-27.** The "extension" labels verdicts fragile beyond the registered range rule. One decision covers the fragility label printed beside H1, H3 and H4.
8. **D-5.** Operational: hold macOS and conda updates until `conf-run-v1` and run on an idle machine (the final dry run logged load averages of 6.75 to 12). Otherwise the run refuses, or needs an amendment.

Parking lot (next tier): D-14 (the Definition of Done's single entry relaxed to one entry plus disclosed ones), D-17 (the design's "corrected after unsealing" sentence is narrower than the plan's), D-29 (H4b's all-targets rule), D-33 (W5 all-or-nothing), D-47 (flag 5; low stakes, since CONF19 holds no truncated origin).
